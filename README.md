# BasedMute

Client-side chat mute for World of Warcraft Classic / Forever.

Add guild names or keywords, and BasedMute hides chat from matching players on your screen only. Nothing is sent to the server, and your ignore list isn't touched. The guild list starts empty.

## Features
- Mute by guild name (matches any part of the name) or by character name
- Filter words out of chat
- Auto-decline guild, group, duel and trade invites from muted players (optional)
- Warnings when you whisper or invite someone on your list
- Guild whitelist for exceptions
- Share your lists with friends, your group or your guild; they choose whether to accept

## Install
Download the latest release zip and extract the `BasedMute` folder into
`World of Warcraft\_classic_\Interface\AddOns\`, or add this repo's URL in WowUp.

## Usage
`/bmute` opens the options. `/bmute guild add Name` mutes a guild. `/bmute help` lists every command.
